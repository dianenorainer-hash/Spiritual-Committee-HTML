About the Project

Objectives

  1. Explain important concepts related to healthy living and the electromagnetic spectrum.
  2. Apply demand and supply concepts to everyday products and food choices.
  3. Present information using clear and meaningful HTML pages.
  4. Practice responsible and ethical digital citizenship.

Purpose

The purpose of this project is to combine learning from different subjects into one meaningful digital educational resource. It aims to present important concepts related to the electromagnetic spectrum, healthy living, smart consumer choices, literature, and personal reflection in an organized and engaging way. Through this website, the project also encourages the application of classroom knowledge to real-life situations, promotes responsible decision-making, and highlights the connection between learning and the Sustainable Development Goals.

Target Audience

The website is intended for students, teachers, and other visitors who want to learn about the topics presented in the project. It provides accessible and organized information that can help students understand the lessons and activities included in the different sections. Teachers and other visitors may also use the website as a simple educational resource for exploring topics related to health, science, economics, literature, digital citizenship, and responsible living.
