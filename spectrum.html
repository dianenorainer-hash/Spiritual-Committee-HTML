EM Spectrum

The Electromagnetic Spectrum

-The electromagnetic spectrum is the range of electromagnetic radiation, organized by frequency and wavelength.

Wave Type Frequency Range Common Practical Applications
Radio Waves 3 kHz to 300 GHz Radio and television broadcasting
Microwaves 300 MHz to 300 GHz Cooking food and satellite communication
Infrared Radiation 300 GHz to 400 THz Remote controls and thermal imaging
Visible Light 400 THz to 800 THz Lighting and photography
Ultraviolet (UV) 800 THz to 30 PHz Sterilizing equipment and detecting counterfeit money
X-Rays 30 PHz to 30 EHz Medical imaging and detecting bone fractures
