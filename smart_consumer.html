Demand and Supply

Demand: The quantity of a product that consumers are willing and able to buy at different prices.

Supply: The quantity of a product that producers are willing and able to offer at different prices.

Smart Consumer Tips

  1. Compare prices before buying.
  2. Check product quality and quantity.
  3. Read labels and product information.
  4. Separate needs from wants.
  5. Plan a budget before shopping.
