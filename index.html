This project connects science, health, economics, literature, and digital citizenship through a collection of learning materials and performance tasks.

Team Introduction

The Spiritual Committee of Grade 9 Chartres presents this digital project as part of our 1st Semester Performance Tasks. Through this project, we share our learning and outputs in different areas, including the electromagnetic spectrum, healthy living, smart consumer choices, literature, and personal reflection. This website showcases our work, ideas, and understanding throughout the project. It also reflects the knowledge and skills we have developed through our different activities and learning experiences. Through the different sections of this website, we hope to present our outputs in a clear, creative, and meaningful way while connecting our lessons to real-life situations and the Sustainable Development Goals.
